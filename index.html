II. Process Thermodynamics

Defining the optimization boundary. Productivity (Material Removal Rate, $Q_w$) is strictly bounded by the maximum permissible contact temperature ($\theta_{crit}$) to prevent metallurgical burn.

Jaeger's moving heat source theorem establishes the nonlinear relationship between specific energy, feed rate ($v_w$), and depth of cut ($a_e$):

$$ \theta_m = \frac{1.13 \cdot q_w \cdot \alpha^{1/2}}{k_w \cdot v_w^{1/2} \cdot l_c^{1/2}} \le \theta_{crit} $$

Material Removal Rate ($Q_w$) drives productivity:

$$ Q_w = v_w \cdot a_e \cdot b $$

Where $b$ is the grinding width. The controller must maximize $Q_w$ without violating $\theta_m \le \theta_{crit}$.

Max Permissible Feed ($v_w^*$) 50.0 mm/s

III. Continuous-Time NMPC Formulation

The C/GMRES algorithm traces the roots of the KKT conditions ($\dot{F}(U,x,t) = -\zeta F(U,x,t)$) continuously, avoiding explicit matrix inversion and rendering receding-horizon optimization viable for machine tool control.

$$ \min_{u} J = \frac{1}{2} x(t+T)^T P x(t+T) + \int_{t}^{t+T} \frac{1}{2} \left[ \|x(\tau) - x_{ref}\|^2_Q + \|u(\tau)\|^2_R \right] d\tau $$

Tuning the ratio of State Penalty ($Q$) to Control Effort Penalty ($R$) fundamentally alters the aggressiveness of the calculated optimal trajectory approaching the strict thermal barrier.

High Q prioritizes reaching max temperature limit rapidly.

High R severely penalizes large actuator torque/feed variations.

IV. Swarm Metaheuristics (COA)

The Coyote Optimization Algorithm provides 1kHz, derivative-free local null-space optimization. It calculates high-frequency variations ($\Delta u$) to reject instantaneous stochastic disturbances (e.g., workpiece hard spots) before the NMPC horizon recalculates.

$$ soc_c^{p,t+1} = soc_c^{p,t} + r_1 \cdot (\alpha - soc_c^{p,t}) + r_2 \cdot (\text{median} - soc_c^{p,t}) $$
